# Gold Copywriting Anchors (Mimic Rhythm & Mechanism, Never Copy Verbatim)

## 1. Kotler (Offer & Clarity)
- Anchor: "Payments infrastructure for the internet. 7 lines of code. 2.9% + 30c. No monthly fees."
- Rule: State category, friction level, and exact terms in <30 words.

## 2. Godin (Polarizing Positioning)
- Anchor: "Built for small teams who want to ship and go home at 5 PM—not for managers who love Gantt charts."
- Rule: Explicitly name the anti-audience and the shared enemy.

## 3. Ogilvy (Fact > Adjective)
- Anchor: "At 60 mph, the loudest noise in this Rolls-Royce comes from the electric clock."
- Anchor (Tech): "1,000 songs in your pocket."
- Rule: Ban hype adjectives. Prove the claim with 1 concrete number or technical detail.

## 4. GaryVee (Social Hook & Rhythm)
- Anchor: "You don't have a marketing problem. You have a 'nobody knows you exist' problem. Stop tweaking button colors. Post what you built raw."
- Rule: First line <12 words. Staccato rhythm. Conversational, blunt, zero corporate polish.

## 5. Neil Patel (SEO & Utility Funnel)
- Anchor: "Most teams waste 40% of their token budget on uncached prompts. Here are 4 patterns tested across 10M requests—plus a free calculator below."
- Rule: Kill warm-up intros. Lead with quantified pain + exact takeaway + utility CTA.
